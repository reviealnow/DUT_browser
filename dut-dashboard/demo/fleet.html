Fleet

Backhaul figures are the last capture, not a live feed: reading one occupies that DUT's serial console, so nothing here refreshes on its own.
Two directions, two measurements. A node's uplink is read with iwconfig on its Managed VAP; the children are read with wlanconfig on the backhaul AP, which only ever answers the downward question. A root cannot name its own backhaul VAP from its own console — it is identified from the Parent BSSID a child reports, which is why Capture all reads nodes before roots.
